# #15 Overlap between train and held-out prompts

- Set out to: fix a leak found in the #11 review: 4 of 200 held-out prompts also appeared among the 2,000 training rows (issue #15 body).
- Cause: finance-alpaca repeats prompts and `make_splits` did not remove them.
- Found: 7,587 of 55,835 length-filtered rows (13.6%) are duplicate prompts (PR #17 body; `docs/decisions.md`, "De-duplicate prompts before splitting").
- Decision: drop repeated prompts (first kept) before the seeded shuffle, not just the 4 rows. This also guards DPO and eval.
- Cost: only 2 of the old 200 held-out prompts survive the reshuffle, so Part 1 numbers are not comparable with the re-run (same sources).
- Side effect: finance share of the references fell from 23.4% (scratch, before) to 12.9% (`outputs/rubric_references/v1/summary.json`).
- Files: `src/post_training/data/finance.py`, `tests/test_data.py`. The re-run records `duplicates_dropped` 7,587 in `outputs/sft/summary.json`.
